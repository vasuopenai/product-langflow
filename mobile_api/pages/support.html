Questions

Which stores show prices and aisles?

Kroger-family stores in the United States, such as Kroger and Harris Teeter. Prices and aisles come from Kroger and can differ from the shelf.

Why does a product say "No store listing"?

The store's chain has no record of that barcode. "Not stocked here" means the chain lists it but your store doesn't carry it.

I scanned a product and it wasn't found.

Thanks! We look it up in public product sources and review it before adding it, so it may take a little while to appear.

Do I need to share my location?

No. Location only helps find the store you're in. You can choose a store yourself on the Store tab.
